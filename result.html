Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is CareerConnect?

CareerConnect is a student placement portal.

2. How is eligibility checked?

Eligibility is checked on the basis of CGPA.

3. What is the minimum CGPA for TCS?

The minimum CGPA required is 7.

4. Can I apply for multiple companies?

Yes, students can check eligibility for different companies.

5. Does this portal guarantee placement?

No, it only helps students check their eligibility.
